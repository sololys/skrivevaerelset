1. Executive Prolegomena: The Pathology of Frictionless Systems & Causal Inertia

1.1 Diagnosing the P=NP Illusion and Frictionless State Propagation

Modern national and digital infrastructure is suffering from a profound strategic vulnerability: the architectural presumption of frictionless, costless state propagation. For over five decades, computing paradigms and distributed engineering models have operated under a "fail-open" ethos, attempting to operationalize the implicit dogma that information can exist and mutate free from physical mass-energy constraints—a structural fallacy referred to as the P=NP illusion. By systematically removing physical resistance, transmission latency, and material counter-forces in pursuit of instantaneous signaling, fail-open architectures decouple digital state transitions from the physical invariants that historically preserved systemic stability. When state propagation is abstracted away from material cost, digital networks permit unconstrained signaling that overpowers biological, cognitive, and cybernetic carrying capacities.
