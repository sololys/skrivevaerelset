## 1. The Pre-Commit ($W_{pre}$) vs. Post-Commit ($W_{post}$) Boundary

### 1.1 Dual-Phase Sealing Architecture and the Realization Boundary

In high-consequence cybernetic architectures and fail-closed realization systems, establishing an unyielding boundary between unverified computational intention and irreversible physical execution is an operational imperative. Digital environments inherently risk entering **"digital cataplexy"**—a failure mode wherein frictionless processing allows raw proposals to collapse directly into physical state transitions without passing through structural validation.
